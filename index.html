How It Works

1. Select Your Source

Choose an app window or display from the session list. Preview instantly to confirm your capture.

2. Configure Output

Enable Syphon or NDI. Adjust transforms, crops, or scaling in the output sheet for perfect framing.

3. Go Live

Start the session. Monitor health status and active outputs from the refined header and status bar.

4. Integrate Seamlessly

Your Mac visuals appear as sources in OBS, Resolume, or any compatible tool—ready for mixing into your workflow.

FAQ

What protocols does SpectraBridge support?

Syphon for local macOS workflows and NDI for network distribution. Both deliver low-latency video feeds.

Is it compatible with my tools?

Works with OBS, Resolume, TouchDesigner, vMix, and any Syphon/NDI receiver. macOS native, optimized for Apple Silicon.

Do I need extra setup?

No complex routing required. Grant permissions once, and sessions run reliably every time.
